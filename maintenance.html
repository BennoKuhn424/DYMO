Maintenance & repairsOfferings

Minimise downtime, stay aligned with safety requirements, and respond fast when breakdowns occur.

Preventative maintenance

Scheduled maintenance helps reduce unexpected failures and supports compliance with the Occupational Health and Safety Act and relevant industry standards including SANS 1545 where applicable. We structure programmes around your equipment, usage, and duty rating.

On the tools

  • Shaft-top and machine-room inspections, adjustments, and lubrication
  • Controller, safety circuit, and door-operator checks
  • Replacement of worn parts with quality components from trusted suppliers
  • Documentation suitable for audits, insurers, and facilities teams
  • 24-hour call-out when passengers are stranded or safety is at risk

24-hour emergency service

When lifts stop, we provide rapid call-out response. We use quality parts through trusted supplier relationships to support durability and compliance after repairs.

Expert technicians

Our technicians undergo continuous training to stay current with equipment, codes, and industry developments—so your assets are serviced by people who understand both legacy and modern systems.

Why proactive maintenance matters

Well-maintained lifts are safer for passengers, less disruptive for tenants, and less costly over the lifecycle than repeated emergency interventions alone.

Compliance & documentation Clear records for audits, insurers, and building management.
Predictable budgets Planned visits reduce surprise spend compared with crisis-only models.
Asset longevity Correct lubrication, adjustment, and part replacement extend service life.
