About us

Founded in 2006 — interior lift design, upgrades, and refurbishments with a personal touch.

Founded in 2006, DYMO specialises in interior lift design, upgrades, and refurbishments. We are a boutique company focused on delivering personalised, high-quality service during and after installations.

Our reputation is built on superior workmanship, competitive pricing, solid business ethics, and sustainable growth through repeat clients.

We combine on-site discipline with in-house fabrication so that panels, frames, and metalwork are produced to exact tolerances and finished to match your specification.

Workshop & facility — Alrode

Our offices and workshop are set up for heavy fabrication and organised material storage: high ceilings, industrial lighting, and dedicated areas for cutting, bending, welding, and polishing.

That capacity supports fast turnaround on stainless and mild steel work, from one-off repairs to full cab packages.
