Lift interiorModernisation

Upgrade older lifts with new design, technology, and components—without always replacing the entire system.

Lift interior modernisation is the process of upgrading an old or outdated elevator system with new technology, improved design, and advanced components to enhance safety, reliability, efficiency, and user experience. It offers a cost-effective alternative to complete replacement and helps buildings meet modern standards and sustainability goals.

Typical projects combine new wall and ceiling finishes, durable flooring, LED lighting, updated car and landing fixtures, and—where required—control upgrades for smoother, more efficient operation.

Why buildings choose modernisation

  • Refresh the passenger experience without the cost of a full new lift
  • Improve hygiene and ease of cleaning with modern surface materials
  • Reduce energy use with LED lighting and efficient drive / control strategies
  • Stay closer to current safety and code expectations
  • Support sustainability goals by extending asset life

Key aspects

Interior modernisation in detail

We focus on three pillars: how the cab looks, how it holds up to daily use and cleaning, and how it performs for passengers.

Aesthetic improvements

  • Wall panels upgraded to contemporary designs
  • New flooring materials for look and durability
  • Energy-efficient LED lighting
  • Stylish handrails for support and finish

Hygiene & durability

  • Smart-surface laminates and anti-scratch finishes
  • Surfaces easier to clean and maintain
  • Stainless steel kick plates to protect doors

Improved functionality

  • Smart control systems for ride quality and efficiency
  • Enhanced door sensors and communication
  • Modernisation aligned with safety and building codes
