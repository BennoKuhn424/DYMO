Lift interior modernisation

Safety, reliability, and a better passenger experience

Modernisation upgrades older lifts with improved design, technology, and components—enhancing safety, reliability, efficiency, and user experience. It is a cost-effective alternative to full replacement and helps buildings meet modern standards and sustainability goals.

Explore modernisation

What modernisation can deliver

  • Updated wall panels, flooring, lighting, and handrails for a contemporary cab
  • Durable, easy-clean surfaces and stainless kick plates where doors wear
  • Modern controls, displays, and touch panels for clearer operation
  • Better ride quality and energy use through smarter control systems
  • Enhanced door sensors and communication for passenger safety
  • Alignment with current building and safety expectations
