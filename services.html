OurServices

A wide range of elevator services to meet commercial, industrial, and residential needs.

At DYMO interiors & upgrades, we offer comprehensive lift and elevator services including the following:

  • Full lift interiors with contemporary designs and premium finishes.
  • Minor installations including mirrors, flooring, and handrails.
  • Repairs and re-cladding of doors and frames for improved aesthetics and durability.
  • Splayed frames and re-spraying of doors and subframes.
  • Polishing of doors and panels for a refined appearance.
  • Installation of modern buttons, digital displays, and touch panels.
  • Specialised goods hoist installations for industrial and retail environments.
  • New paraplegic lifts, dumbwaiters, and home lifts.

From premium full-cab interiors to targeted upgrades—mirrors, flooring, hoists, and accessibility lifts—we scope each job for your building type, traffic, and budget.

Passenger elevator car decoration

Standard and optional car interior specifications. Every cab is built to your shaft dimensions — car walls, ceilings, flooring, and handrails can be specified to suit your building.

Finishes shown are indicative. Ceilings, landing doors, handrails, and floors can be specified separately — see the series below. Send us your cab dimensions or drawings and we will quote the exact specification, or view Our Work for completed installations.

Ceiling series

Suspended ceiling designs with integrated lighting. Specified with the car wall finish.

Landing door series

Landing door finishes to match the car interior, from plain hairline stainless to etched and gold designs.

Control panels & landing fixtures

Car operating panels, hall lanterns, and landing call stations. Faceplates are matched to the car wall finish.

Handrail series

Four handrail profiles, specified per cab and finished to match the car wall.

Floor series

Marble, granite, and patterned stone floor options. PVC is supplied as standard — the finishes below are available on request.
